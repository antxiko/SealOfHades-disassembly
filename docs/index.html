A shoot-'em-up on foot, going up: Leon shoots, breaks the ground blocks to get what they keep, watches his air and his power, and at the end of each of the six scenes beats the stone guardian. If he finds The Stone of Wisdom in another slot, he starts stronger. The title comes from running the ROM, checked against openMSX point by point; the game in GIF, played in openMSX; the twelve tunes that play, from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1986 - 32 KB cartridge (header at 0x4000) - MSX - Dump fcc6b6b9...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The title comes from running the ROM and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the CASIO intro, the title, the controls, AIR, POWER and the shots, the blocks, the hearts and GAME OVER.
  • The scenes: the six scenes, their screens, the enemies, the guardian and the ending.
  • The sprites: the hero in four sprites, his shots, every enemy and the explosions put together from the ROM tables.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: The Stone of Wisdom in another slot, TAB and CAPS, a demo that plays the header, leftover texts.
  • Secrets and bugs: what was searched for: the keys and the other cartridge, the high score set to zero past 9,999,990, the cursor keys and joystick mixed, and the extra life at 30,000 checked.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the cartridge, the BIOS it uses, the loop, the scroll, the RAM and the tools.
